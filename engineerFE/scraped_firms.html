Questions

Hiring an engineering firm

When should I hire a firm rather than an individual engineer?
When the work spans several disciplines, when it needs internal checking and a named discipline lead, when the programme is long enough that one person's availability is a risk, or when your procurement requires a legal entity with insurance and certifications. For a bounded single-discipline task, an individual is usually faster and cheaper.
What does company verification cover?
Legal entity registration, authorised representatives, and compliance documents. Firms can additionally hold certification verification for their management systems and insurance verification for professional indemnity cover. Each is a separate verified field, not one badge.
Does the firm's verification cover every engineer on their team?
No. Company verification confirms the entity. Individual engineers hold their own identity, education, registration and certification verification, and a firm's team roster shows each person's status. Ask who specifically will do your work.
Can a firm sign off drawings for my jurisdiction?
Only if the firm — or the named professional within it — holds the registration your jurisdiction requires. Platform verification is not a licence. Many firms work across jurisdictions by preparing the package and engaging a locally registered professional for sign-off; ask how they intend to handle it before you contract.
